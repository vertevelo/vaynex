Who's on the other side of the coffee

I'm Vaynex.

A boom-bap producer with one rule: no compromises. Dusty drums, hand-picked samples and the raw, head-nodding energy of the golden era.

After a deliberate restart as a beatmaker, I build songs with independent rappers who want substance, not filler. Next to boom bap there's a darker cinematic trap side, for rappers who need to let something out.

Most producers send a link and wait. I'd rather know who I make music with. That's what the coffee is for: you talk, I listen. If it clicks, we figure out the next step together.

Want to see how I work first? I make my beats on camera, from the first sample to the drop, on @vaynex061.
