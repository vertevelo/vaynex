About

The story behind the beats.

Vaynex is a boom-bap producer from Switzerland with one rule: no compromises. The sound is built on dusty drums, hand-picked samples and the raw, head-nodding energy that defined the golden era of hip-hop.

After a deliberate restart as a beatmaker, focused on analog over algorithm and craft over shortcuts, Vaynex keeps releasing beats and collaborations for independent artists who want substance, not filler. Alongside Boom-Bap, a darker Cinematic Trap side exists for rappers who need to let something out rather than sort it out.

Open for collabs, custom beats and features. If you rap with intent, we'll get along.
